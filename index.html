Welcome to Abdul Sattar Trader's & Furniture Mart

Managed directly by brothers Zahid Abdul Sattar and Shahid Abdul Sattar, our workshop provides practical, customized furniture and complete woodwork services in Kot Radha Kishan and surrounding towns in District Kasur.

We build every piece to match the exact dimensions of your rooms and the strength demanded by Pakistani household use. Whether you need a brand-new 7-seater drawing room sofa, replacement of sagging foam with authentic high-density Master foam, custom-fitted bedroom almirahs, or modular kitchen cabinets, you discuss timber, plywood, and fabrics directly with the craftsmen who build them.

Our facility also operates an active heavy carriage trolley band saw (trolli wala araa) where customers bring whole tree trunks to be cut into rafters and planks according to their exact measurements, alongside dry firewood sold honestly by weight on our platform scale (tarazu).
