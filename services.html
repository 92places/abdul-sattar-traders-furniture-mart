Sofa Re-Foaming on Frame

Master Foam Installation (Sofa Re-Foaming)

Our Master foam service specializes in stripping away old, deteriorated, or sagging foam right down to the sofa’s bare wooden skeleton frame (lakri ka dhancha). Our craftsmen inspect the joints, re-tension the base webbing, and fit brand-new, authentic high-density Master foam layers and contour cushions to restore showroom firmness and comfort.

Common Customer Requests & Options:

  • Stripping old foam down to bare wooden frame
  • Authentic high-density Master Molty foam
  • Re-tensioning base springs & webbing straps
  • Custom deewan and mattress contouring

Firewood & Weight Scale

Firewood Sales & Platform Scale

We supply cut firewood in various quantities for domestic heating, tandoor, cooking, and other lawful purposes. Sourced directly from our sawmill operations, the wood pieces and offcuts are stacked in our workshop yard and weighed on our traditional platform scale (tarazu) so customers get verified, honest weight per maund or kilo.

Common Customer Requests & Options:

  • Domestic firewood for winter heating and stoves
  • Wood supply for cooking and tandoors
